Google Drive Link Guide

To make PDFs viewable directly inside this portal:

  1. Upload your official PDF document to Google Drive.
  2. Right-click the file → select Share → Share.
  3. Under General Access, change setting to "Anyone with the link".
  4. Click Copy link and paste it directly into the PDF URL field in the Admin Panel.
The system automatically formats your link into a direct preview embed link!
